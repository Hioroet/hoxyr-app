Pricing FAQ

What counts as an AI action?

An AI action is any request that invokes the model against your connected workspace — for example drafting a reply, summarizing a thread, or generating a task. Browsing and viewing content does not count.

Can I switch between monthly and annual?

Yes. You can change billing frequency from your account settings at any time. Changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.

Who handles my payment and taxes?

Paddle is our Merchant of Record, so it securely processes every payment and calculates applicable taxes at checkout. See our Refund Policy for details.
