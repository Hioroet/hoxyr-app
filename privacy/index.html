Privacy Policy

How Hoxyr collects, uses, and protects your data. We never use your content to train public models, and your payment data is handled securely by Paddle.

Last updated: November 1, 2026

1 Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to operate the Hoxyr workspace and the integrations you enable. This falls into three categories:

Account information

Name, email address, organization details, and authentication data you provide when signing up.

API-connected tools data

Content from connected integrations (Slack, Notion, Google Workspace, GitHub, etc.) accessed via OAuth to power your workspace.

Payment data

Billing is handled securely by Paddle, our Merchant of Record. Hoxyr does not store full card numbers.

We also collect basic usage and diagnostic data (such as device type and feature interactions) to keep the Service reliable and secure. We do not track you across third-party advertising networks.

2 How We Use Your Information

We use your information strictly to operate the workspace for you. This includes authenticating your account, connecting the integrations you authorize, generating AI responses using your connected context, securing the Service, and providing support.

We never use your data to train public AI models. Your connected content is used solely to serve your own workspace sessions and is not repurposed to train any publicly available foundation model, ours or anyone else's. Aggregated, de-identified analytics may be used to improve service reliability, but never to identify you or train models on your content.

The paid Pro plan ($24/month or $19/month billed annually) is processed entirely by Paddle as Merchant of Record. Paddle handles payment, tax, and billing compliance; Hoxyr receives only the minimum data needed to confirm your subscription status.

3 Data Security & Retention

We protect your data using end-to-end encryption in transit and encryption at rest. Access to production systems and customer data is restricted through role-based access controls, with the principle of least privilege enforced across all internal tooling.

We retain your account and connected-data caches only for as long as needed to provide the Service, or as required by law. When you delete an integration or close your account, associated data is removed from active systems within 30 days and from backups within 90 days, except where retention is legally required.

Payment data is stored and secured by Paddle in accordance with PCI-DSS standards. Hoxyr does not store complete payment instrument details.

4 GDPR / CCPA Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under the EU/UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and similar laws. Subject to applicable law, you may exercise the following rights:

  • A Access. Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you and the integrations connected to your workspace.
  • D Deletion. Request that we delete your personal data and disconnect integrations, subject to legal retention obligations.
  • M Modification. Correct inaccurate or incomplete personal data, or update your account information at any time.

You also have the right to object to or restrict certain processing, withdraw consent, and lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. To exercise any right, contact our privacy team at privacy@hoxyr.app. We respond to verified requests within 30 days.

Privacy questions?

For any privacy or data-protection inquiry, contact our dedicated privacy team at privacy@hoxyr.app. For general support or billing, write to support@hoxyr.app.
