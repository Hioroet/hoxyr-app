Refund Policy

Clear, fair terms around trials, renewals, and refunds. All billing and refunds are processed by Paddle, our Merchant of Record.

Last updated: November 1, 2026

1 Free Trial Period

Every new Hoxyr account includes a 14-day free trial of the Pro plan, with no credit card required to start. The trial gives you full access to Pro features and integrations so you can evaluate the workspace before committing.

You can cancel anytime during the trial directly from your account settings, or simply do nothing — we will not charge you and your account will not be automatically converted to a paid plan without your explicit consent. After the trial ends, you remain on the Free tier unless you choose to upgrade to Pro.

2 Refund Eligibility

The Pro plan is priced at $24/month, or $19/month when billed annually. Subscriptions are non-refundable once the billing period begins, because access is granted immediately for the full cycle.

We make two clearly defined exceptions:

  • Confirmed technical bugs. If a verifiable bug prevents you from using the Service and we cannot resolve it within a reasonable timeframe, we will refund the affected billing period.
  • Accidental renewals. If an automatic renewal catches you by surprise, you may request a refund within 48 hours of the renewal charge, provided 0 AI actions have been used in the new billing period.

Refund requests outside these exceptions are reviewed on a case-by-case basis and are not guaranteed. To avoid unwanted charges, remember that you can cancel at least 24 hours before renewal as described in our Terms of Service.

3 Processing Refunds

All refunds are managed via Paddle, our Merchant of Record, which also handles payment processing, tax, and compliance for every Hoxyr transaction. Once a refund is approved, it is credited back to your original payment method within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank or card issuer.

To request a refund, email support@hoxyr.app with your account email and the transaction reference from your Paddle receipt. Please include a short description of the issue so we can verify eligibility and process the request promptly.

Approved refunds apply only to the disputed billing period. Active subscriptions remain in effect until the end of the paid cycle unless you cancel separately.

Need help with a refund?

For refund requests and billing support, contact support@hoxyr.app. For privacy-related inquiries, contact privacy@hoxyr.app.
